FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need a credit card to sign up? +
No. All plans start with a 90-day free trial — no credit card required. Your workspace is provisioned the moment you verify your business email.
Can I use Ledgio for my hotel and restaurant together? +
Yes. The Hospitality plan links room billing, restaurant tabs, laundry, and other ancillary charges to a single guest folio that closes at checkout — all in one workspace.
Is my data safe if Ledgio is multi-tenant? +
100% — each business has its own schema-isolated database. No two tenants share tables or queries. Your data is also encrypted at rest and in transit, with daily backups.
Can nonprofits and schools use Ledgio? +
Yes. The Schools & NGOs plan includes fund accounting, term-based fee collection with SMS parent alerts, donor grant tracking, and NaSIA/GES-aligned reporting formats.
Can I upgrade my plan later? +
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or add users at any time from your account settings. All your data and historical records are preserved across plan changes.
Is there a setup fee? +
No setup fees, no onboarding charges. Your workspace is provisioned instantly and you can be recording transactions within minutes of registering.
Does Ledgio support Mobile Money payments? +
Yes. MoMo, MTN Mobile Money, Vodafone Cash, and AirtelTigo are all supported as payment methods and can be reconciled directly in your Ledgio workspace alongside bank transactions.
