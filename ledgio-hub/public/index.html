🏨 Hotels & Guest Houses

Run Your Property. Know Every Cedi.

Ledgio's hospitality module replaces the POS with a full property management system — reservations, room management, guest billing, housekeeping, and night audit — all connected to a real double-entry ledger.

  • Room types with per-night rates, capacity, and amenity lists
  • Reservation system with availability checks — no double-booking
  • Guest folio: room nights + extra charges (F&B, laundry, minibar)
  • Check-in / Check-out with automatic invoice generation
  • Housekeeping board: Dirty → Clean → Inspected status flow
  • Night audit: occupancy %, RevPAR, and room revenue by date
  • All revenue posts automatically to Room Revenue (GL 4000)
  • Guest deposits tracked as liabilities, cleared on check-out
