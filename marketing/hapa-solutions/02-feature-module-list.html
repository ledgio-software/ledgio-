Ledgio is cloud software for business management — covering accounting, POS, inventory, and sales in a single system. Multi-tenant and multi-branch: every business gets its own isolated workspace. Installs on any device as a PWA — Windows PC, Mac, Android, or iPhone. No app store. No IT department.
