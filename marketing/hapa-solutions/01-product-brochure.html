The Problem We Solve

What schools are dealing with

Manual ledgers, disconnected spreadsheets, and cash handling with no audit trail create risk for schools of every size.

Problem
Fees collected — but where is the money?
Ledgio tracks every payment, partial or full, and tells your bursar exactly who has paid, who hasn't, and the live outstanding balance at any moment. No more end-of-term surprises.
Problem
Canteen attendants pocketing the difference
Every tuck shop sale generates a receipt the attendant must print before the till unlocks for the next sale. The boss sees a full receipt audit log daily. Zero leakage.
Problem
Payroll done by hand every month
Automate salary computation, SSNIT and PAYE deductions, and send payslips digitally. Headmaster signs off in minutes. Statutory-compliant payroll in Ghana.
