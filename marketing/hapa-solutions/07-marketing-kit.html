Ledgio is cloud-based business management software built for companies that buy and sell. It connects your Point of Sale tills, inventory across multiple branches, supplier purchases, customer invoicing, and full double-entry accounting into one seamless system. Every sale at the till instantly updates your stock levels and posts to your accounts — no manual re-entry, no reconciliation headache. Built-in anti-fraud controls protect every transaction. Manage unlimited branches and tills from one dashboard. Ledgio installs on any device — Windows PC, Mac, Android, or iPhone — in seconds, with no app store and no IT setup required. Partner guidelines

Dos & Don'ts

Follow these guidelines in all marketing. Hapa Solutions is authorised to promote and sell Ledgio — these rules protect both the partner and the brand.

✓ Do
  • Call Ledgio "business management software" or "cloud software"
  • Say Ledgio installs on any device via PWA
  • Say there is a free Starter plan during early access
  • Say Ledgio connects POS, inventory, and accounting
  • Say Ledgio works on Windows, Mac, Android, and iPhone
  • Identify yourself as an "Authorised Ledgio Partner" or "Hapa Solutions — Ledgio Partner"
  • Use all copy on this page verbatim or with minor personalisation
  • Include your own contact details alongside Ledgio's email
✗ Don't
  • Call Ledgio a "website" or "web app"
  • Promise specific uptime percentages or SLA terms not in writing
  • Quote pricing other than what is in the approved materials
  • Claim features that have not been confirmed as live in the software
  • Use Ledgio's name or logo in a way that implies Ledgio and Hapa Solutions are the same company
  • Make comparisons to named competitors without prior approval
  • Offer discounts or pricing modifications without authorisation
  • Share this marketing kit with parties outside your sales team
