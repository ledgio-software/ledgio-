Getting Started

5 questions

Ledgio is cloud software for business management — combining accounting, Point of Sale (POS), inventory, invoicing, and reporting in one system.

Instead of juggling separate tools for your till, your stock, and your books, Ledgio connects them all — so every sale at the counter automatically updates your accounts and stock levels in real time.

Getting started is straightforward:

  • Contact ledgioapp@gmail.com or your Hapa Solutions representative to set up your account
  • You'll receive login credentials for your business workspace
  • Your Hapa onboarding specialist walks you through the initial setup — adding your products, your team, and your shop(s)
  • You're live and trading, typically within a day

Yes. Ledgio's Starter plan is free during early access — giving you POS, basic inventory, and sales invoicing for one branch with up to three users, at no cost.

When you're ready to add more branches, more users, or the full accounting suite, you can upgrade to the Business or Enterprise plan.

Ledgio installs directly on your device as a Progressive Web App (PWA) — a modern way to install software without going through an app store or calling IT.

On any device — Windows PC, Mac, Android, or iPhone — open your browser, go to Ledgio, tap Install or Add to Home Screen, and the software is on your device with its own icon. No app store. No download file. No IT department. Updates happen automatically in the background.

Most businesses are up and running within one to two days. The timeline depends on how many products you need to load and how many branches you operate.

Hapa Solutions provides guided onboarding to get your product catalogue, user accounts, and till configured as quickly as possible.

Platform & Devices

Ledgio works on any device with a modern browser:

  • Windows PC — Chrome, Edge, or Firefox
  • Mac — Safari or Chrome
  • Android phone or tablet — Chrome
  • iPhone or iPad — Safari

On any of these, you can install Ledgio as a PWA — it adds an icon and opens full-screen like a native app, without going through an app store.

A Progressive Web App (PWA) is a way to install software directly onto your device without going through an app store or downloading an installer file. You tap "Install" in your browser and Ledgio becomes a full application on your device — its own icon, its own window, works like any other software.

This matters because:

  • Install on any device your team already has — no new hardware needed
  • Software updates happen automatically — you always have the latest version
  • Add a new till or computer in seconds — install Ledgio, log in, start selling

Ledgio is cloud software and requires an internet connection to record sales, update stock, and sync data across branches in real time.

We recommend a reliable Wi-Fi or mobile data connection at each till point. A basic mobile data plan is sufficient — Ledgio is designed to work well even on slower connections.

Yes — Ledgio is built for teams. Multiple cashiers, accountants, and managers can all be logged in simultaneously across different devices and branches. Each person sees only what their role permits, and all data syncs in real time.

Features

5 questions

Yes. Ledgio supports unlimited branches and shops under one account (on Business and Enterprise plans). Each branch has its own tills, its own stock levels, and its own user assignments — while the owner gets a consolidated view across all locations.

The Ledgio POS supports the following payment methods:

  • Cash with automatic change calculation
  • Card — logged as card payment
  • Mobile Money — MoMo, M-Pesa, and other mobile payment platforms

The cashier selects the payment method at checkout, and the receipt and daily cash-up report reflect the split.

Yes. Ledgio includes a full double-entry accounting engine:

  • Chart of accounts (assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses)
  • Bank reconciliation
  • Manual journal entries
  • Profit & Loss statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow report
  • VAT / tax summary for filing

Every POS sale and purchase automatically posts to the accounts — so your books are always up to date without manual re-entry.

Ledgio has several built-in anti-fraud controls:

  • Force-print receipt gate — a cashier cannot record the next sale until they have opened the receipt PDF for the previous one, preventing the gap between what a customer pays and what gets recorded
  • Receipt audit log — admins and accountants can view every transaction ever made, with date, cashier name, items, amounts, and PDF receipt
  • Void logging — any voided sale requires a reason and records who voided it and when
  • Daily cash-up — expected cash vs. actual cash is reconciled at the end of each shift

Yes. Ledgio's invoicing module lets you create tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive invoices, convert quotes to invoices, and issue credit notes. Every invoice can be downloaded as a PDF and shared with the customer directly.

Security & Data

4 questions

Yes. Ledgio is built on cloud infrastructure with industry-standard security practices:

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
  • Access requires login credentials — sessions time out automatically
  • Every action is logged for audit purposes
  • Regular automated backups ensure your data is never lost

Absolutely not. Ledgio is a multi-tenant platform with strict data isolation. Each business has its own completely separate workspace. No other business — including other Ledgio customers — can ever see, access, or query your data.

This isolation is enforced at the database level on every single query, not just at the application layer.

Ledgio uses role-based access control. You decide what each person can see and do:

  • Admin — full access, including all reports and user management
  • Accountant — accounting, reconciliation, and financial reports
  • Cashier — POS checkout and their own sales history only
  • Viewer — read-only access to dashboards and reports

You assign a role to each team member when you invite them, and can change it at any time.

Your data belongs to you. Before closing your account, you can export your financial reports as PDFs and download your records. Contact ledgioapp@gmail.com to arrange a data export before account closure.
