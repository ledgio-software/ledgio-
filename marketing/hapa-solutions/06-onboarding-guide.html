1
Account Setup
Configure your business workspace before anything else
Admin only
1
Receive and test your login credentials
Hapa Solutions will send your admin email and temporary password. Log in, change your password immediately, and confirm you can access the dashboard.
2
Set your business details
Go to Settings → Business Profile. Enter your business name, address, phone number, and upload your logo. These appear on invoices and receipts.
3
Configure currency and tax
Set your base currency (e.g. GHS, NGN, KES) and your VAT / tax rate. You can set different tax rates for different product categories later.
4
Add your branches / shops
Go to Settings → Branches. Add each physical location where you sell or store goods. Each branch gets its own tills and stock levels. 💡 You can add more branches at any time after going live
5
Set up your Chart of Accounts
Ledgio provides a default chart of accounts ready to use. If your accountant has a preferred structure, set it up now. Enter opening balances if you are migrating from an existing system.
2
Add Your Team
Invite staff and get Ledgio installed on every device
Admin
1
Invite team members by email
Go to Settings → Users → Invite. Enter each person's email address. They will receive a link to create their password and join your workspace.
2
Assign roles
Choose the correct role for each person: Admin (owner / manager), Accountant (finance team), Cashier (till staff), or Viewer (read-only). A person can only see what their role permits.
3
Install Ledgio on every device
On each PC, phone, or tablet, open the browser, go to your Ledgio link, and tap Install or Add to Home Screen. Ledgio installs as software on the device — its own icon, its own window. 💡 Works on Windows, Mac, Android, and iPhone — no app store required
4
Confirm each person can log in
Ask each team member to log in and verify they see the correct screens for their role before moving on to loading products.
3
Load Your Products
Build your product catalogue and set opening stock
Admin or Accountant
1
Create product categories
Group your items into categories (e.g. "Phones", "Accessories", "Stationery"). Categories make searching faster at the POS and help with sales reports.
2
Add your items
For each product enter: name, SKU / barcode, selling price, cost price, tax rate, and category. Items can be added one by one or imported from a spreadsheet (CSV). 💡 Ask your Hapa representative for the CSV import template
3
Set opening stock levels
For each item, enter how many units you currently have at each branch. This gives Ledgio an accurate starting point for stock tracking.
4
Set low-stock alert thresholds
For items you reorder regularly, set a minimum stock level. Ledgio will alert you when any item falls below its threshold at any branch.
4
Configure Your POS
Set up tills, assign cashiers, and run a test sale
Admin
1
Create a till for each checkout point
Go to POS → Tills → New Till. Give each till a name (e.g. "Main Counter", "Branch 2 Till 1") and assign it to the correct branch.
2
Assign cashiers to tills
Each cashier user is linked to a till when they open their shift. Confirm each cashier knows which till they will use.
3
Run a test sale end-to-end
Log in as a cashier, search for a product, add it to the cart, select a payment method, and complete the sale. Confirm the receipt PDF opens and the stock count decreases by one. 💡 The cashier must open the receipt PDF before recording the next sale — this is a security control
4
Run a test cash-up
At end of the test shift, go to POS → Cash Up. Enter the cash counted in the till and confirm the system's expected amount matches. Review the daily summary report.
5
Go Live
Train your team and start trading on Ledgio
All staff
1
Train your cashiers
Walk each cashier through: searching for items, processing a sale, handling cash and mobile money payments, opening the receipt PDF, and doing end-of-shift cash-up. Hapa Solutions can run this training session for you.
2
Train your manager / accountant
Walk through the daily reports, receipt audit log, stock levels view, and how to add new products or users. For accountants, cover the accounting modules and bank reconciliation.
3
First day of live trading
Start your first real day with Hapa support on standby. Record all sales through Ledgio from day one — do not run parallel paper systems alongside.
4
Review end-of-day reports
At close of business, the admin or accountant reviews: daily sales summary, cash-up report, stock movements, and the receipt audit log. Flag anything unexpected to your Hapa representative. ✅ Congratulations — you are now running fully on Ledgio

Daily & Monthly Routines by Role

Quick reference for what each role does in Ledgio on a regular basis.

Cashier

Each Shift

Start of shift
  • Open Ledgio, log in, select your till
  • Count opening float and record it
During trading
  • Search product by name or SKU
  • Add items, select payment method, complete sale
  • Open receipt PDF before next customer
  • Void a sale only with a valid reason
End of shift
  • Count cash in till
  • Submit cash-up report in Ledgio
  • Log out
Admin / Manager

Daily & Weekly

Each day
  • Review daily sales summary
  • Check cash-up reports from all tills
  • Check stock levels — reorder if below threshold
  • Review receipt audit log for anomalies
Each week
  • Review weekly sales by product / branch
  • Check for any voided transactions
  • Add new products or update prices as needed
  • Review any low-stock alerts
Accountant

Monthly

Weekly
  • Record supplier invoices and payments
  • Reconcile mobile money and card settlements
End of month
  • Bank reconciliation — match statement to Ledgio
  • Review Profit & Loss statement
  • Review Balance Sheet
  • Generate VAT / tax summary for filing
  • Export reports to PDF for records
  • Lock the period to prevent retrospective edits
