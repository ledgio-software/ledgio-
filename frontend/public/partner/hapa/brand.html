Type scale rule: Headings in Cormorant Garamond 600–700. Body, labels, captions, and UI text in Outfit 400–600. Never use Cormorant Garamond for body paragraphs or small text — below 16px it loses legibility. Never use Outfit for large display headings.

04 — Voice & Tone

How Ledgio Sounds

Ledgio speaks like a confident, experienced business partner — direct, clear, and respectful of the reader's intelligence. No hype. No jargon. Specific beats clever.

Direct
Say exactly what it does
Specific
Real numbers, real names
Confident
No hedging or apologies
Human
Warm but not casual
