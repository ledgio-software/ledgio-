## What a Referral Partnership Involves

A Referral Partner's role is straightforward: **identify, introduce, and qualify** prospective businesses in your network who would benefit from Ledgio. You share accurate information about the platform using materials we provide, submit the referral through our agreed process, and Ledgio handles the demonstration, onboarding, technical support, and customer relationship from that point forward.

**You may:**
- Present Ledgio's approved features, pricing and value proposition
- Qualify prospects and collect basic business information for the referral
- Share approved Ledgio marketing materials
- Introduce prospects to Ledgio's sales process
- Maintain your own commercial relationship with referred clients for consulting and your own services

**You may not:**
- Make commitments regarding customisation, enterprise pricing, or technical integrations without prior written approval
- Conduct independent product demonstrations unless mutually agreed and trained
- Represent yourself as a Ledgio employee or as having authority to bind Ledgio contractually
- Collect payments on Ledgio's behalf

**Attribution is protected:** once you submit a referral through the agreed process, it is attributed to you. Ledgio will not circumvent attribution by accepting the same customer through another channel.
